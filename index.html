A new technology

Brazzein: a sweet protein designed by nature

2,000×

Up to 2,000 times sweeter than sucrose (500× against a 10% sugar solution).[1]

6.5 kDa

Only 54 amino acids: one of the smallest sweet proteins.[1]

98 °C

Still tasted very sweet after 2 hours at 98 °C.[3]Taste tests in buffers from pH 2 to 8.

The question is not whether it works. The question is how to produce it competitively.

New biotechnology paradigm

Lactuca sativa: a lettuce biofactory

  1. Design

    Plant-optimized brazzein gene and expression cassette.

  2. Grow

    Transient expression in hydroponic lettuce through agroinfiltration.

  3. Purify

    A heat step that leverages brazzein's thermostability, confirmed by ELISA and Western blot.

Adapted to hydroponic systems A familiar food crop
